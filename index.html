Risk Classification

Five risk tiers under the EU AI Act

The scanner determines which tier applies and generates the full requirements checklist automatically.

🚫
PROHIBITED
AI systems posing unacceptable risk to fundamental rights. Banned from the EU market entirely.
Article 5
⚠
HIGH RISK
Systems in critical sectors: employment, infrastructure, law enforcement, education, finance, migration.
Annex III
⚡
LIMITED RISK
Chatbots, synthetic media generators, emotion inference systems. Transparency obligations apply.
Article 50
🤖
GPAI MODEL
Foundation models and LLMs. Documentation, training data transparency, and copyright compliance required.
Articles 51-55
✓
MINIMAL RISK
Spam filters, AI in games, recommendation engines. No mandatory obligations. Voluntary codes encouraged.
Article 95
